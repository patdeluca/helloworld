For the man in the Yankees hat

Dad at Yankee Stadium ⚾

★ About My Dad ★

There he is — Yankees gear on, game in front of him, not a single problem in the world. This is him in his natural habitat, and it pretty much captures everything I love about him in one shot.

💖 What Makes Him Great

He doesn't need a lot of noise. He just shows up, fully present, genuinely happy — and somehow that's always been enough to make everyone around him feel exactly the same way.

Thanks for being the kind of dad who taught me that the best moments in life are the simple ones. A good seat, a good game, good company. You've given me more of those than I can count.

★ Love you, Dad. Go Yankees. ★

Always in your corner!!
